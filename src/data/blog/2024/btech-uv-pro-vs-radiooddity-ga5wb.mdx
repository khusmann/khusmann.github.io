Despite being sold by different companies under different model numbers, the BTech UV-Pro, RadioOddity GA-5WB, and Vero VR-N76 are essentially the same radio&mdash;just rebranded and slightly tweaked by their respective OEMs.

Even though they're the same hardware, however doesn't mean they're the same radio, however. I first got the RadioOddity GA-5WB, then later the UV-Pro so I could compare them side-by-side. In my testing I found a number of differences between the radios, including the accessories they provide, their transmit power, firmware capabilities, and GPS performance.

**TL;DR: If you're looking at these radios and considering which one to get, get the UV-Pro.** It is more reliable, has more features, and its support teams are more responsive / knowledable. (So much so that I returned my GA-5WB and got another UV-Pro). Although I never tested a VR-N76, I believe it is similarly configured to the GA-5WB.

### Summary

The biggest difference between what's included in the box is that the UV-Pro does not include a K1 adapter cable, and [sells it separately for $22](https://baofengtech.com/product/gmrs-pro-k1-adaptor).

That said, although this is a point in the GA-5WB favor, I think the other advantages of the UV-Pro (as you will see in future sections) outweigh it.
